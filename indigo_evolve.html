The SV Group Story

S. Vinodkumar Diamonds is one of India's leading manufacturers and distributors of polished diamonds. Co-founded by Mr. Sumatilal Shah and Mr. Vinod Shah, the company was established in 1978 with humble beginnings and has grown through family unity, unwavering work ethics, and a relentless pursuit of quality.

Today, SV Group operates across 7 countries globally, and its name is synonymous with the finest manufactured diamonds. The group engages in direct procurement from mines, advanced CVD/HPHT diamond cultivation, diamond manufacturing, and precision diamond-studded jewellery creation.

With decades of industry leadership, SV Group is a genuine partner in its clients' success. The Responsible Jewellery Council certification (renewed through 2029) reflects the group's enduring commitment to ethics, safety, and sustainable luxury.
