Our Heritage

Backed by a
45-year legacy

IDI Jewels is the dedicated lab-grown diamond division of the SV Group. While the origin of the stones has evolved through technology, the craftsmanship, precision, and global reach remain rooted in our multi-decade heritage.

We combine state-of-the-art CVD and HPHT growing technologies with our world-class jewelry manufacturing infrastructure in Mumbai and Asia. The result is unparalleled quality at scale.
