Who We Are

Indigo Jewellery is the fine jewellery division of S. Vinodkumar Diamonds Pvt. Ltd. (SV Group) — one of India's most respected names in diamond manufacturing. Founded in 1978 by Mr. Sumatilal Shah and Mr. Vinod Shah, the group has become synonymous with quality, integrity, and global reach.

Our jewellery operations commenced in 1999 with a unit in SEEPZ, Mumbai, and have grown into a multi-factory, multi-continent operation serving top-tier retailers worldwide. We are not a consumer brand — we are a manufacturing and wholesale partner to the world's finest jewellery retailers.

Our Story

Crafting brilliance

Indigo Jewellery is the jewellery company of SV Group, and an associate of S. Vinodkumar Diamonds Pvt. Ltd. — a global leader in diamond trading, manufacturing, and jewellery creation with over 50 years of experience.

The SV Group Story

S. Vinodkumar Diamonds is one of India's leading manufacturers and distributors of polished diamonds. Co-founded by Mr. Sumatilal Shah and Mr. Vinod Shah, the company was established in 1978 with humble beginnings and has grown through family unity, unwavering work ethics, and a relentless pursuit of quality.

Today, SV Group employs over 5,000 people in 7 countries, and its name is synonymous with the finest manufactured diamonds globally. The group engages in direct procurement of rough diamonds from mines, diamond manufacturing, and diamond-studded jewellery — from mine to market.

With decades of industry leadership, SV Group is not just a trusted advisor but a genuine partner in its customers' success. The Responsible Jewellery Council certification (renewed through 2029) reflects the group's commitment to ethics, safety, and sustainability.

The Industry Context

Why sustainability
is not optional

The jewellery industry sits at a unique intersection of nature, craft, and commerce. Diamonds and precious metals are gifts of the earth — and their extraction carries a real environmental and human cost. As a responsible player in this industry, we take that seriously.

Retail buyers, end consumers, and institutional partners increasingly require — and deserve — transparency. ESG credentials are no longer a "nice to have"; they are a prerequisite for doing business with the world's leading retailers, brands, and investors.

At Indigo and across the SV Group, we do not treat sustainability as a compliance exercise. It is embedded in how we source, how we manufacture, how we employ, and how we give back — reflecting values that our founders instilled from day one in 1978.

Cultural Heritage

Preserving places of peace

The SV Group has funded the construction of a dharamshala — a Jain pilgrimage rest house — at Palitana, Gujarat. The facility houses 34 rooms and four halls with a canteen, providing safe and comfortable accommodation for thousands of pilgrims every year.

Our factories are built to the standards of the international diamond industry — minimising environmental impact at every stage of the manufacturing process.

Responsible Waste Management

Metal filings, swarf and polishing residues are carefully collected, segregated and recycled through certified processes — ensuring precious metals never enter landfill.

Water Conservation

Chemical treatment baths and water-intensive polishing processes are managed with closed-loop systems where possible, reducing consumption and preventing contamination of local water sources.
